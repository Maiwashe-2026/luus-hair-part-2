Wig After Care Guide

Keep your Luu's Hair wig looking beautiful, soft and luxuious with the right care.

Follow these simple steps to maintain your wig's beauty and longevity.

💞Daily Care

  • Handle your wig gently.
  • Store it on a wig stand.
  • Use a wide-tooth comb or wig brush.
  • Keep synthetic wigs away from heat.

🫧Washing Instructions

  • Wash your wig every 10–15 wears.
  • Use wig shampoo and conditioner.
  • Soak in cool water and gently swish.
  • Let the wig air-dry on a stand.

Styling Tips

  • Use low heat on human hair wigs only.
  • Avoid heavy oils and sprays.
  • Protect your wig at night with a satin bonnet.
  • Trim ends and adjust straps when needed.
