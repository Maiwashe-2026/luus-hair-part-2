## 1. Organisation overview
### Business name: 
 Luu’s Hair is a luxury frontal wig company created to offer premium quality hair solutions for women interested in protective styling, convenience and confidence. What started as a small boutique supplier has grown into a well-respected brand, known for its craftsmanship, transparency and personalized customer service. Lu’s Hair specializes in high end frontal wigs, closures and hair accessories. Our focus is on quality assurance and customer education.  
 
### Mission:
Lu’s Hair is dedicated to empowering women thru luxury frontal wigs that enhance beauty, build confidence and allow for true self-expression. The brand’s mission is to inspire every woman to feel bold, elegant and unapologetically herself with thoughtful design and premium craftsmanship.

### Vision: 
Luu’s Hair aims to become the luxury wig brand leader in South Africa with innovative spirit, superior product quality and exceptional customer experience. The brand aims to bring premium craftsmanship and forward-thinking design to set the national standard for excellence in the luxury hair industry. 

### Target Audience: 
Luu’s Hair caters to a diverse demographic of women aged 18-45, from wig novices to protective style clients, beauty enthusiasts and women searching for the best hair solutions. This audience wants quality, convenience and luxury, making them the perfect customer for a brand that is all about high end frontal wigs and great service.
## Website Goals and Objectives
Luu’s Hair seeks to boost its digital presence thru the creation of a luxury-oriented website to improve brand credibility and business expansion. The website will be a key element to help improve online sales of frontal wigs and accessories by providing customers with a smooth and attractive shopping experience. It will also be an educational platform that offers quality content on wig installation, maintenance and styling to help both beginners and experienced users. Interactive features, inquiry systems and effective communication channels will help to improve the website to enhance customer engagement and to develop long term relationships. Furthermore, by incorporating testimonials, reviews and the presentation of premium products, you will gain trust, enhance the brand’s image and establish Luu’s Hair as a leading provider of luxury frontal wigs in South Africa.
### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs): 
The success of Luu’s Hair’s website will be measured through a set of key performance indicators that indicate customer behaviour and overall business growth. Such metrics can include monthly website traffic, which shows the reach of the brand, and the conversion rate, which shows how well the visitors turn into paying customers. Average order value will be used to measure the buying patterns and the volume of customer inquiries will be used to gage the level of engagement and interest in what the brand has to offer the repeat purchase rate will indicate customer loyalty and the engagement of the educational content will show how well the website is assisting users with tutorials, guides, and wig-care resources.
## Proposed Website Features and Functionality
The website created would have structured, functionality-driving pages aimed to reflect luxury while catering for customer needs. Features: Home page The hero banner- to capture the customers in one-screen and showcase the core brand. Highlighted products- featuring top sellers. 
 About Us page - showcase the brand’s narrative, mission and values and introduce to the founder. Products page- showcase excellent photography for luxury products, prices, brief descriptions and filters such as length, lace-type and density to aid customers to make an informed purchase. Enquiry page - Form to customize an order from our website. Contact page- Showcase multiple shop details with an integrated map as well as forms for easy inquiry.
